Who we are

Quebec-built marine services

Laurentide Marine is a Quebec company serving ship owners, agencies, charterers and port authorities across the Great Lakes, the St. Lawrence and Saguenay, and the Maritimes.

We work to food-grade inspection standards, move fast on tight port windows, and handle wash-water and waste responsibly — so vessels turn around clean and on time.
